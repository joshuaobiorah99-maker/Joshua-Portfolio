ABOUT ME

Building Ideas Into Web Experiences

I'm Joshua, a web developer. My journey started with curiosity about how websites are made. I began learning HTML and CSS on Mimo, then moved to VS Code and JavaScript and started building real projects, like my Bean & Brew Cafe website. The more I built, the more I knew this is something I love and want to pursue as a career.

I want to work with small and large businesses, from cafés, restaurants and shops to companies and personal brands, building professional websites for them. I'm also working towards more advanced features like online ordering, WhatsApp integration, databases and backend systems.

What sets me apart is that I don't just write code. I focus on websites that are useful, professional and solve real problems, with equal attention to design and functionality. I keep learning and improving with every project I build.

I'm also studying data processing, with a diploma due in December, which helps me build websites that handle information clearly and accurately.
